What stillwater is

stillwater is the open-source Software 5.0 operating layer that underpins Solace City. It implements the canon-update RSI paradigm: the system gets smarter by updating its own canon (skills, recipes, persona library) — not by retraining model weights.

Why canon-update RSI

Weight-update RSI assumes the model is the seat of intelligence. Canon-update RSI assumes the substrate is. Brains shrunk roughly 10 percent over the last 3000 years while human capability multiplied — because we externalized intelligence into shared canon (writing, math, libraries, code).

Solace bets on the same path: a substrate that holds skills, personas, conventions, and verification ladders, and lets any LLM run inside it without fine-tuning.

How it relates to the rest of the portfolio

stillwater is the open-source kernel. solaceagi.com is the commercial driver that funds stillwater development. IF Theory provides the physics substrate. phuc.net documents the journey.
